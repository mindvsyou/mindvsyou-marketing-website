MCQ · 2025 · 1 mark

Electrolysis of water is a decomposition reaction. The mass ratio (MH : MO) of hydrogen and oxygen gases liberated at the electrodes during electrolysis of water is :

  1. 8 : 1
  2. 2 : 1
  3. 1 : 2
  4. 1 : 8
Show answer

Answer: D – 1 : 8

AI Explanation:
• Electrolysis of water produces hydrogen and oxygen in a 2:1 volume ratio, but by mass, oxygen (molar mass 32) is much heavier than hydrogen (molar mass 2).
• ✓ Option (d): The mass ratio MH : MO works out to 1 : 8, since 2 volumes of H2 (mass 4) form against 1 volume of O2 (mass 32), giving 4:32 = 1:8.
• Why the others are wrong: (a) inverts the ratio; (b) and (c) confuse the volume ratio (2:1) with the mass ratio.

Reason (R) : Decomposition of organic matter into compost is an exothermic process.

  1. Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
  2. Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
  3. Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.
  4. Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Show answer

Answer: B – Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A).

AI Explanation:
• Decomposition reactions generally require energy input (heat, light or electricity) to break bonds, making Assertion true; composting is also a genuine example of an exothermic decomposition, making Reason true too.
• ✓ Option (b): Both statements are true, but the Reason does not explain the Assertion, since composting is an exception, not the general basis for Assertion.
• Why the others are wrong: (a) wrongly links R as the explanation; (c) and (d) incorrectly mark one statement as false.

Assertion-Reason · 2025 · 1 mark

Assertion (A) : Concentrated nitric acid is diluted by adding water slowly to acid with constant stirring.
Reason (R) : Concentrated nitric acid is easily soluble in water.

  1. Both, Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
  2. Both, Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, and Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
  3. Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.
  4. Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
Show answer

Answer: D – Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.

AI Explanation:
• Acid is always added to water slowly (not water to acid), because the process is highly exothermic and can cause spattering otherwise — so the Assertion's stated method is reversed and incorrect.
• ✓ Option (d): Assertion (A) is false (the correct method is acid added to water), but Reason (R) is true — concentrated nitric acid is indeed soluble in water.
• Why the others are wrong: (a), (b), (c) all treat the Assertion as true, but the stated method is actually unsafe.

LA · 2024 · 5 marks

Common salt is a very important raw material as many compounds of industrial use can be prepared from it. Explain, giving chemical equations, the method of preparation of washing soda from sodium chloride. List four industrial/domestic uses of washing soda.

Show answer

Answer:
• Concentrated solution of sodium chloride reacts with ammonia and carbon dioxide to obtain sodium hydrogen carbonate and ammonium chloride.
• NaCl + NH3 + CO2 + H2O → NaHCO3 + NH4Cl
• When sodium hydrogen carbonate is heated strongly, sodium carbonate is obtained.
• 2NaHCO3 --Heat--> Na2CO3 + CO2 + H2O
• Sodium carbonate is dissolved in water to obtain washing soda.
• Na2CO3 + 10H2O → Na2CO3·10H2O
Uses:
• In glass, soap and paper industries.
• Manufacture of borax.
• As a cleaning agent for domestic purposes.
• For removing permanent hardness of water.

AI Explanation:
Given: Sodium chloride (NaCl) is the raw material for washing soda.
• Step 1: NaCl + NH3 + CO2 + H2O → NaHCO3 + NH4Cl (Solvay process forms sodium hydrogen carbonate).
• Step 2: 2NaHCO3 --heat--> Na2CO3 + CO2 + H2O (heating gives sodium carbonate).
• Step 3: Na2CO3 + 10H2O → Na2CO3·10H2O (crystallisation gives washing soda).
∴ Uses of washing soda: (1) glass, soap and paper industries; (2) manufacture of borax; (3) as a domestic cleaning agent; (4) for removing permanent hardness of water.

SA · 2024 · 3 marks

Why is the conversion of ethanol to ethanoic acid an oxidation reaction ? Name the oxidising agent used in this conversion. Write chemical equation for this oxidation reaction. How is this reaction different from the reaction in which ethanol burns in the presence of oxygen ?

Show answer

Answer:
• Oxygen is added to ethanol to produce ethanoic acid, which is why it is an oxidation reaction.
• Oxidising agent: Alkaline potassium permanganate (or Acidified potassium dichromate).

LA · 2025 · 5 marks

(a) Define the term "Functional group of a carbon compound". Write the formula of functional group present in (i) propanol and (ii) propanone.
(b) Name the process of conversion of (i) ethanol to ethene and (ii) ethanol to ethanoic acid. Write the reaction equation stating the conditions required for each of the reactions to occur.

Show answer

Answer:
(a)
• Functional group: The element (or group) replacing hydrogen in a carbon compound is called a heteroatom; these heteroatoms confer special properties to the compound and are known as functional groups.
• (i) Functional group in propanol: –OH (Alcohol)
• (ii) Functional group in propanone: >C=O (Ketone)
(b)
• (i) Dehydration: C₂H₅OH →(443 K, Conc. H₂SO₄, excess) C₂H₄ + H₂O (ethanol → ethene)

AI Explanation:
(a)

(b)
• (i) Ethanol converts to ethene by dehydration: heating with excess concentrated sulphuric acid at 443 K removes water, C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O.
• (ii) Ethanol converts to ethanoic acid by oxidation: heating with alkaline potassium permanganate oxidises the alcohol group to a carboxylic acid, C₂H₅OH → CH₃COOH.

MCQ · 2025 · 1 mark

Consider the following food chain :
Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Eagle
If the amount of energy available at third trophic level is 50 kJ, the available energy at the producer level was :

  1. 0.5 kJ
  2. 5 kJ
  3. 500 kJ
  4. 5000 kJ
Show answer

Answer: D – 5000 kJ

AI Explanation:
Given: Energy at 3rd trophic level (Frog) = 50 kJ; only 10% of energy transfers between successive trophic levels.
Energy at 2nd level = 50 × 10 = 500 kJ
Energy at 1st (producer) level = 500 × 10 = 5000 kJ
∴ Available energy at producer level = 5000 kJ.
✓ Option (d): 5000 kJ is correct.
Why the others are wrong: The other values come from dividing instead of multiplying, or from using the wrong number of trophic-level steps.

  2. Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A).

  4. Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.

SA · 2025 · 3 marks

(a) 'In a food chain energy flow is unidirectional.' Give two reasons for the given statement.
(b) If 10,000 J energy is available at the producer level, how much energy will be available to the secondary consumers ? Give reason to justify your answer.

Show answer

Answer:
(a)
• The energy captured by plants does not revert to solar input, and the energy that passes to herbivores does not revert back to autotrophs.
• As energy moves progressively through the trophic levels, it is no longer available to the previous level.
• The energy available at each trophic level gets progressively diminished due to loss of energy at each level. (any two)
(b)
• 100 J
• Autotrophs (10000 J) → Primary consumer (1000 J) → Secondary consumer (100 J)
• Only 10% of the energy of the organic matter of the previous trophic level is transferred to the next trophic level (10% law).

AI Explanation:
(a)
• Energy captured by producers never reverts back to the sun or to the previous trophic level once it moves forward through the food chain.
• Energy available at each trophic level diminishes progressively, so it cannot flow backward.
(b)
• Only 10% of energy transfers at each trophic level (10% law): 10,000 J → 1,000 J (primary consumers) → 100 J (secondary consumers).
∴ Secondary consumers receive 100 J.
