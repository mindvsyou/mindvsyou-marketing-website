How many years of PYQs should you solve for CBSE Class 10 Science?

5 min read · Updated Sep 2026 · By the MindVsYou team

Solving five years of papers sounds thorough. Solving fifteen sounds heroic. But more years is not automatically better, and the honest answer depends on how much time you have and how the paper has changed.

Quick answer

Solve the last 3 to 5 board years. Treat the most recent years as your main practice because they reflect the current paper style. Go further back only for extra questions on a chapter you are still weak in, and only after you have finished the recent papers properly.

How many years of PYQs are enough?

For most students, the last three to five years is the sweet spot. Three years gives you a reliable feel for the paper. Five gives you enough variety that patterns become obvious. Beyond that, returns fall quickly: older papers were set under different patterns, and time spent on them is time not spent reviewing your mistakes.

Why do recent years matter more?

CBSE has shifted steadily towards competency-based, application-style questions, including case-based questions and Assertion-Reason MCQs. Recent papers reflect that style. An older paper heavy on direct recall can make you feel more prepared than you really are. Check the current paper structure and prioritise papers that match it.

What is a simple plan based on the time you have?

Time until boardsSuggested plan
6+ monthsChapter-wise PYQs from the last 4 to 5 years as you finish each chapter; 2 to 3 full papers at the end.
3 monthsChapter-wise from the last 3 to 4 years; full papers weekly in the final month.
4 to 6 weeksLast 3 years, chapter-wise for weak chapters first; alternate days of full papers.
Under 2 weeksMost recent 2 to 3 papers as timed papers; review every mistake rather than adding older papers.

Is it better to solve fewer years thoroughly or more years quickly?

Fewer years, thoroughly. A paper you have attempted, reviewed and re-attempted teaches more than three papers skimmed. If you find yourself finishing papers without knowing why you lost marks, you are ready for depth, not more volume. Read common PYQ mistakes to see how that trap works.

When do older PYQs make sense?

Use them as top-up practice when a chapter still feels shaky after you have exhausted recent questions, or when you are ahead of schedule and want more variety. Skip older questions that clearly belong to a syllabus portion that has since been removed; always check against your current-year syllabus.

Frequently asked questions

Are 10 years of PYQs too many?

For most students, yes. Older papers may not match today's pattern, and the time is better spent reviewing and re-attempting recent questions.

Is solving only last year's paper enough?

One paper is too narrow. You will see only one selection of topics and one mix of question styles. Three to five years gives a far more reliable picture.

Should I solve PYQs of other subjects the same way?

The same principle applies: recent years, chapter-wise first, full papers later. MindVsYou currently covers Class 10 Science, with Mathematics coming soon.

Do sample papers count as PYQs?

No. Sample papers are practice papers released by CBSE; PYQs are actual board exam papers. Both are useful. Use sample papers to understand the current pattern and PYQs for real questions.
