What MindVsYou Is

Real board questions, turned into practice that remembers you

MindVsYou is an online practice platform for CBSE students, currently focused on Class 10 Science. It turns CBSE previous year questions (PYQs) from a stack of PDFs into an organised, interactive practice experience at quiz.mindvsyou.io.

🗂️

A structured PYQ bank

Real CBSE previous year questions, sorted by chapter, subtopic and question type, covering 2022–2025 board papers.

🧭

Practice that keeps track of you

See how you're doing chapter by chapter, and what needs another look, without keeping a notebook of mistakes.

How It Works

Five steps from chapter to confidence

A simple, chat-style flow that feels like working with a tutor, not sitting a test.

1

Choose a chapter

Pick any Class 10 Science chapter, and narrow it to a subtopic if you want to focus.

2

Practise question by question

Questions arrive one at a time, so you stay focused on the one in front of you.

3

Get feedback immediately

Objective questions are marked instantly with an explanation. For written questions, attempt your own answer first, then compare it with the model answer.

4

See where you stand

Your practice builds a chapter-wise picture of your accuracy, so you know which chapters are strong and which need work.

5

Fix what you missed

Questions you got wrong come back for revision, so mistakes become the next thing you practise.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is MindVsYou?

MindVsYou is an online CBSE exam-practice platform, currently for Class 10 Science. Students practise real previous year board questions (PYQs) chapter by chapter at quiz.mindvsyou.io, get instant feedback, and see which chapters and questions need more revision.

Is MindVsYou free?

Yes. MindVsYou is currently in beta, and the first 100 students get full access to every feature for free. No credit card required. You can also try it as a guest with no account.

Which class and subject does MindVsYou cover?

CBSE Class 10 Science, using real previous year board questions. Mathematics is coming soon.

Do I need to download an app?

No. MindVsYou works in any browser on any device at quiz.mindvsyou.io. There is nothing to install.

Which years of previous year questions are covered?

MindVsYou currently covers CBSE Class 10 Science previous year questions from 2022 to 2025.

Does MindVsYou include case-based questions?

Yes. Case-based questions are practised alongside MCQs and very short, short and long answer questions, the way they appear in the CBSE paper.

Can I download previous year papers or practise on paper?

Yes. You can download a whole previous year paper with its answer key, and chapter-wise printable sets of MCQs and written questions with answers, for practising on paper instead of a screen.

How is MindVsYou different from a PDF of previous year questions?

A PDF is a static document. MindVsYou organises questions by chapter and subtopic, marks objective questions instantly with explanations, lets you compare written answers with a model answer, tracks your progress chapter by chapter, and brings the questions you missed back for revision.

Is MindVsYou good for CBSE board exam revision?

Yes. Chapter-wise PYQ practice, instant feedback and revision of your mistakes are built for CBSE board exam preparation. It works alongside your textbook, school and teachers.

Can parents use MindVsYou to follow a child's progress?

MindVsYou shows chapter-wise accuracy and the questions that still need revision, which helps parents see which chapters are going well and where their child needs support.

Can teachers recommend MindVsYou to students?

Yes. Teachers can recommend it as a focused PYQ practice resource for CBSE Class 10 Science. Students can try it free, with no account needed.

Is Mathematics available on MindVsYou?

Not yet. MindVsYou currently covers CBSE Class 10 Science, and Mathematics is coming soon.

How do I report a wrong question?

You can flag any question in one tap from the practice screen, and it will be reviewed and fixed.

Who is behind MindVsYou?

MindVsYou is operated by Sparqity Private Limited. You can reach the team at hello@mindvsyou.io.
