Important Class 10 Science MCQ concepts, chapter by chapter

8 min read · Updated Sep 2026 · By the MindVsYou team

CBSE Class 10 Science covers Physics, Chemistry and Biology in one paper, and with limited revision time not every idea deserves equal attention. Here is a practical map of the concepts board MCQs keep coming back to, and how to revise each area efficiently.

Quick answer

Board MCQs concentrate on reaction types and reactivity in Chemistry, numericals and ray diagrams in Physics, and labelled processes in Biology. Revise by chapter, practise each concept in more than one format, and always re-attempt the questions you got wrong. Confirm the exact topics against your current-year syllabus.

Which Chemistry concepts matter most for MCQs?

Chemical Reactions and Equations and Acids, Bases and Salts are foundational. MCQs often test whether you can identify a reaction type (combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement, redox) or predict a product, rather than recite a definition. Balancing equations and recognising precipitates, gas evolution and colour changes are common threads.

Metals and Non-metals follows a similar logic: questions frequently hinge on the reactivity series and predicting what happens when a specific metal meets a specific solution or acid. Carbon and its Compounds tests naming, functional groups and the properties of ethanol and ethanoic acid, which are easy marks once the pattern clicks.

The efficient way to revise this block is not rereading reaction types; it is working through enough questions that you recognise a reaction type on sight.

Which Physics concepts matter most for MCQs?

Light: Reflection and Refraction and The Human Eye and the Colourful World combine concepts with numericals (mirror and lens formula, magnification, power of a lens) and are a common source of diagram-based questions: image formation, sign conventions, defects of vision, dispersion and scattering.

Electricity is numerical-heavy: Ohm's law, series and parallel combinations, heating effect and power. Fluency with formulas and units matters more here than almost anywhere else in the paper. Magnetic Effects of Electric Current tests field patterns, the right-hand rule and Fleming's left-hand rule through simple diagram-based reasoning.

Which Biology concepts matter most for MCQs?

Life Processes is the densest Biology chapter: nutrition, respiration, transportation and excretion each carry their own vocabulary and diagrams, and MCQs often ask you to label or interpret a figure. Control and Coordination (nervous system and hormones) and How do Organisms Reproduce? follow the same pattern. Heredity brings short numerical-style reasoning around Mendelian ratios. Our Environment is comparatively lighter but appears reliably in case-based questions on food chains and ecosystems.

How should you prioritise MCQ revision with limited time?

  • Start with your weakest chapters, not the ones above by default. Your own accuracy data beats any generic ranking. See how to find your weak areas.
  • Practise each concept in more than one format. The same idea shows up as an MCQ, a short answer and a case; do at least two of these.
  • Redo what you got wrong. A wrong MCQ you never revisit teaches you nothing; one you return to a few days later fixes the gap.

Then apply the method in how to prepare for Science MCQs and avoid the traps listed in common MCQ mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

Which chapters have the most MCQs in Class 10 Science?

CBSE does not fix a per-chapter MCQ count, and it varies by year. Rather than guess, practise every chapter and use your own accuracy to decide where to spend extra time.

Which Class 10 Science chapters are easiest to score in?

It differs by student. Many find Our Environment and parts of Life Processes and Heredity scoring, but your best guide is your chapter-wise accuracy.

Are diagram-based MCQs common in Class 10 Science?

Yes. Ray diagrams, circuits and biology figures regularly appear, so practise reading and labelling them rather than only memorising text.
