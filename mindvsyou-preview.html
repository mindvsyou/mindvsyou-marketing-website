Our Story

Built for the student who already has a phone

We believe every Class 10 student deserves great board exam prep — not just those who can afford coaching centers or study apps.

Why I Built This

The problem I saw

Millions of Class 10 CBSE students prepare for the most important exam of their early lives with limited support. Quality coaching costs money. Study apps are distracting. Physical books are expensive.

But almost every student — even in smaller cities — already has WhatsApp on their phone.

So I built a practice bot that meets students where they already are. No app to download. No new habit to build. Just say Hi and start practicing CBSE-aligned MCQs, case-based questions, and previous year board questions for Maths and Science.

I am building this in India, starting small and listening closely to early users to make this genuinely useful before scaling up.

General

About MindVsYou

The basics — what it is and how it works.

MindVsYou is a WhatsApp-based practice bot for CBSE Class 10 students. You send a "Hi" on WhatsApp and the bot starts sending you CBSE-aligned MCQs and case-based questions. No app download, no account creation needed.
Right now, MindVsYou is designed specifically for Class 10 CBSE students preparing for their board exams. We plan to expand to Class 9 and other boards in the future.
No. MindVsYou works entirely on WhatsApp, which you already have. There is no separate app to download, no website to log in to, and no form to fill out. Just open WhatsApp and say Hi.
Yes — MindVsYou is currently in beta, and the first 100 students get full access to every feature for free, including MCQ practice, case-based questions, and the PYQ revision bank. No credit card required.
Currently we cover Mathematics and Science for CBSE Class 10 — including chapter-wise MCQs, case-based questions, and a dedicated Previous Year Questions (PYQ) bank with revision tracking. More subjects will be added soon.
Using the Bot

How to practice

Step by step — so you know exactly what to expect.

Click the "Try on WhatsApp" button. It opens WhatsApp with our number already added. Just send "Hi" and follow the prompts — you will be practicing within 60 seconds.
The bot sends you a multiple-choice question with options labeled A, B, C, D. Simply reply with the letter of your choice. The bot immediately tells you if you are right or wrong and explains the answer.
Yes. At any point during your practice session, you can type "Menu" to go back to the subject selection screen.
Case-based questions give you a short passage or scenario and ask multiple questions based on it. These now make up a significant portion of the CBSE board paper. MindVsYou drills you on these formats specifically.
PYQ stands for Previous Year Questions — actual questions from past CBSE Class 10 board exams for Maths and Science. The bot quietly tracks every question you get right or wrong, so you can type "Revise" anytime to practice only the ones you got wrong before.
Your phone number is only used to identify your practice session. We do not share, sell, or misuse your number. You can unsubscribe at any time by typing "Stop".
