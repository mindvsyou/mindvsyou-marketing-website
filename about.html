Our Story

Built for the student who already has a phone

We believe every Class 10 student deserves personalised board exam prep — not just a generic watching of youtube videos.

Why I Built This

The problem I saw

Millions of Class 10 CBSE students prepare for the most important exam of their early lives with generic support. But they lack personalised support and live tracking of their progress, achievements and shortcoming.

Almost every student — even in smaller cities — already has a smartphone and internet access.

So I built an online practice tool that meets students where they already are. No app to download. No new habit to build. Just log in and start practicing CBSE-aligned MCQs, case-based questions, and previous year board questions for Maths and Science.

I am building this in India, starting small and listening closely to early users to make this genuinely useful before scaling up.
