CBSE Class 10 Science MCQ practice: how to prepare and what to focus on

7 min read · Updated Sep 2026 · By the MindVsYou team

MCQs look like the easy part of the Science paper: one mark each, four options, no writing. That is exactly why students under-prepare for them. A single MCQ takes seconds, but a week of careless MCQ practice can cost you marks you already knew.

Quick answer

Prepare for Class 10 Science MCQs by practising real board-style MCQs chapter by chapter, checking the reason behind every answer (not just the letter), and tracking accuracy per chapter. Focus on concepts, diagrams, numericals and application, because board MCQs test understanding, not just recall.

What do CBSE Class 10 Science MCQs test?

Section A of the Science paper is made up of around twenty one-mark objective questions, including a couple of Assertion-Reason questions (check the current sample paper for the exact count). Together they sample the whole syllabus, so no chapter is safe to skip. They typically test:

  • Direct concepts: definitions, properties, named processes.
  • Application: which reaction type, which lens or mirror, which organ or hormone fits a situation.
  • Numericals: short calculations using Ohm's law, the mirror or lens formula, power.
  • Diagrams and data: reading a ray diagram, a circuit, a labelled figure or a small table.

How do you prepare for Science MCQs? A five-step method

1. Learn the concept before you drill the question

MCQ practice on an unstudied chapter is guessing with extra steps. Read the chapter once, then practise.

2. Practise chapter by chapter

Ten to fifteen MCQs from one chapter show you quickly whether you actually know it. Mix in several years of board-style questions so that you see the same concept asked in different ways.

3. Say why the right option is right and why the others are wrong

This is the single biggest accuracy upgrade. Board options are usually built from common misconceptions, so each wrong option teaches you something about a mistake you might make.

4. Keep a short error log

One line per miss: chapter, concept, reason (did not know / misread / slipped). Patterns show up within a week. See common Science MCQ mistakes for the usual culprits.

5. Revisit what you missed, after a gap

Come back to missed MCQs after two or three days and again after a week. A question you can answer correctly after a gap is a question you have learned.

How many MCQs should you practise?

There is no magic number, but a steady ten to twenty a day across different chapters for the last few weeks beats a marathon the night before. Quality matters more than count: an MCQ you understood deeply is worth several you clicked through. Combine this with the 15-minute daily practice rule.

Which chapters need the most MCQ attention?

All thirteen chapters can appear, but some reward specific preparation: reaction types and reactivity series in Chemistry, numericals and diagrams in Light and Electricity, and labelled processes in Life Processes. The chapter-by-chapter breakdown is in important Science MCQ concepts.

Frequently asked questions

How many MCQs are there in the CBSE Class 10 Science paper?

Section A of recent Science sample papers has about twenty one-mark objective questions, including Assertion-Reason questions. Always check the current-year CBSE sample paper for the exact structure.

Are Science MCQs easy?

They are short, but not always easy. Many are application-based or diagram-based, and wrong options are built from common misconceptions. Regular chapter-wise practice makes them easier.

Should I guess in MCQs?

CBSE board MCQs carry no negative marking, so never leave one blank. Guess only after eliminating the options you can rule out.
