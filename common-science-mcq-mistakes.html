Common Class 10 Science MCQ mistakes, and how to improve your accuracy

6 min read · Updated Sep 2026 · By the MindVsYou team

Most MCQ marks are lost to habits, not ignorance. You knew the concept; you still picked the wrong option. Fix the habits and your accuracy climbs without studying a single extra page.

Quick answer

The most common Science MCQ mistakes are misreading the question (NOT, EXCEPT, INCORRECT), unit and sign slips, skimming diagrams, changing correct answers and memorising options. To improve accuracy: read the stem twice, check units, eliminate wrong options with a reason, and review every miss.

What are the most common Science MCQ mistakes?

1. Missing NOT, EXCEPT or INCORRECT in the stem

"Which of the following is not a displacement reaction?" is answered wrongly by students who read the first half and spot a displacement reaction. Fix: underline negative words before reading the options.

2. Unit and sign slips in numericals

Focal length in centimetres but object distance in metres; the wrong sign for a concave mirror's focal length; resistance in kilo-ohms. Fix: write units next to every number and apply the sign convention deliberately.

3. Skimming diagrams

Ray diagrams, circuits and biological figures hide the answer in details: where the ray bends, which resistors are in parallel, which arrow points where. Fix: trace the diagram with your pen before reading the options.

4. Falling for the "almost right" option

Board options are designed around misconceptions. Two options can both look plausible. Fix: ask what exact word makes the wrong option wrong (for example, "all" versus "some", "increases" versus "decreases").

5. Changing a correct answer at the last second

Second-guessing hurts more often than it helps when you have no new information. Fix: change an answer only if you can name the specific error in your first reasoning.

6. Memorising the option letter instead of the concept

"It was B" is not knowledge. When the board rewords the question, you lose the mark. Fix: after each question, restate the concept in your own words.

7. Not treating Assertion-Reason as its own skill

Students who know the chapter still lose marks because they merge the two statements into one. Fix: see how to solve Assertion-Reason questions.

How do you improve MCQ accuracy?

  1. Read the stem twice before looking at the options.
  2. Predict the answer before reading the choices, then match.
  3. Eliminate with a reason, not a hunch. If you cannot say why an option is wrong, you have not eliminated it.
  4. Practise by chapter so patterns and misconceptions become familiar.
  5. Log every miss with its reason: concept, misread or slip.
  6. Revisit misses after a gap of two or three days.
  7. Track accuracy per chapter, and practise the lowest ones next. More in how to find your weak areas.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I get MCQs wrong even when I know the concept?

Usually because of misreading (missing NOT or EXCEPT), unit or sign slips, or skimming a diagram. Track the reason for each miss to find your own pattern.

Is there negative marking in CBSE Class 10 MCQs?

No. CBSE board MCQs do not carry negative marking, so attempt every question, using elimination where you are unsure.

How long should I spend per MCQ?

About a minute on average. Spend less on recall questions to save time for numericals and diagrams.
