Getting Started

How to practice

Step by step — so you know exactly what to expect.

Click the "Practice on Web" button on this website. It takes you to quiz.mindvsyou.io where you can log in and start — you will be practicing within 60 seconds.
The bot sends you a multiple-choice question with options labeled A, B, C, D. Simply reply with the letter of your choice — for example, type "B" and send. The bot immediately tells you if you are right or wrong and explains the answer.
Yes. At any point during your practice session, you can type "Menu" to go back to the subject selection screen and choose a different subject.
Case-based questions are a format introduced by CBSE in recent years. They give you a short passage or scenario and ask multiple questions based on it. These now make up a significant portion of the CBSE board paper. MindVsYou specifically drills you on these formats so you are prepared.
PYQ stands for Previous Year Questions — actual questions that have appeared in past CBSE Class 10 board exams for Maths and Science. As you practice these, the bot quietly records every question you got right and every question you got wrong. Anytime you type "Revise", it pulls up only the questions you got wrong before, so your practice time goes straight to your weak spots.
Yes. If you stop mid-session, the bot saves where you left off. You can come back anytime — even days later — and type "Resume" to continue from where you stopped.
On the free plan, you can practice up to 20 questions per day across all subjects. On premium plans, there is no daily limit.
