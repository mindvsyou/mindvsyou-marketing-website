General

About MindVsYou

The basics — what it is and how it works.

MindVsYou is a CBSE Class 10 exam-prep platform. Students practice with real Previous Year Question (PYQ) papers in a chat-style conversational interface — right in the browser at quiz.mindvsyou.io, no app to install.
Right now, MindVsYou is designed specifically for Class 10 CBSE students preparing for their board exams. We plan to expand to Class 9 and other boards in the future.

Setting up your account

Step by step — so you know exactly what to expect.

Enter your name and set a 4-digit PIN. The system generates a unique Student ID for you in the format MVY#### (e.g. MVY0042).

PYQ Practice

Practising with real exam questions

PYQ stands for Previous Year Questions — real questions from past CBSE board exams.

MCQ Practice — answer real past-exam multiple-choice questions one at a time, in a chat format, with instant right/wrong feedback.

Subjective Practice — attempt real past long-answer questions, then reveal the model answer and self-rate how you did.

Revise Mode — go back over questions you previously got wrong (this needs a saved history, so it's for registered students only, not guests).

Full Paper Download — download an actual past exam paper as a PDF, plus its official answer key.
Case-based questions are a format introduced by CBSE in recent years. They give you a short passage or scenario and ask multiple questions based on it. These now make up a significant portion of the CBSE board paper, and appear throughout MindVsYou's MCQ and subjective PYQ practice.
No — you just pick the year and subject. Behind the scenes, MindVsYou randomly hands you one of several equivalent question-paper sets for that year (all sets cover the same syllabus/difficulty), so it doesn't matter which one you get. Every download click can serve a different set.
Registered students get 50 new PYQ questions per day (MCQ + Subjective + Revise combined) — this resets daily. Downloading full paper PDFs doesn't count against this limit. Guests (not logged in) don't hit this daily cap, but the app won't repeat a question you've already seen in that browser.
No — the app tracks which questions you've already been shown (server-side if you're logged in, in-browser if you're a guest) and rotates so you don't repeat them.
Yes. At any point during your practice session, you can go back to the menu and choose a different subject or chapter.

Yes, if you're registered. Your dashboard shows your performance by chapter, with labels like "Critical," "Developing," "Average," and "Strong" to flag where you need the most work. Guests see a sample/preview of what this looks like, not their own data.
