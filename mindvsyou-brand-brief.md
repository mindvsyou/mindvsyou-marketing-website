# MindVsYou — Brand & Product Brief
*Reference doc for writing social media posts, journalist pitches, and articles about MindVsYou. Paste this into the Claude Project's knowledge/instructions.*

## What it is
MindVsYou is a browser-based CBSE Class 10 board exam practice platform. Students practice Maths and Science through MCQs, case-based questions, Assertion-Reason questions, and real CBSE Previous Year Questions (PYQs) from 2022–2026 — chapter by chapter, with instant explanations. No app download required; works at quiz.mindvsyou.io.

- **Company:** Sparqity Private Limited (India)
- **Marketing site:** mindvsyou.io
- **Product/app:** quiz.mindvsyou.io
- **Stage:** Beta — 100 free beta spots, all features included
- **Subjects covered:** Maths & Science, CBSE Class 10 (currently — do not imply other classes/boards/subjects are covered yet)
- **Contact:** hello@mindvsyou.io · Instagram @mindvsyou · LinkedIn /company/mindvsyou-ai

## The problem it solves
Millions of CBSE Class 10 students prepare for boards with generic support — YouTube videos, textbooks, coaching centers — but no personalised tracking of their own strengths and weaknesses. Quality board-exam prep is often gated by city (metro vs. smaller towns) or ability to pay for coaching. Almost every student already has a smartphone; MindVsYou meets them there instead of asking them to build a new habit around a new app.

## Core features — LIVE now (use these specific terms, not vague paraphrases)
Everything currently live sits inside **PYQ Mode** — every question a student sees is sourced from actual real CBSE Class 10 exam papers (2022–2026), not freshly written or generated practice questions. Within PYQ Mode:
- Chapter-wise PYQ practice with instant answers and explanations
- CBSE Class 10 Science & Maths MCQs
- Case-based questions
- Assertion-Reason questions
- Subjective/written-answer questions (very short, short, and long answer), with a model answer to self-rate against
- Auto-tracked revision — questions/answers you got wrong or rated poorly are brought back until they stick
- Downloadable/printable whole previous year papers with answer keys (PDF)
- 100% browser-based — no app download, works on any smartphone

**Do not describe this generically as "practice mode"** — the correct, specific term is **PYQ Mode** or **PYQ practice**. Always name the specific question format too (MCQ, case-based, Assertion-Reason, subjective/written-answer) rather than a vague "practice questions."

## NOT part of current messaging — do not mention
There is a separate, **not-yet-live "Practice Mode"**: the same question formats (MCQ, subjective, case-based, etc.) but made up of *non-PYQ* questions — same style and difficulty as the board exam, but not verbatim from a real past paper. This does not exist in the product yet. Do not imply that MindVsYou offers extra/generated practice questions beyond the actual PYQs — every question today is a real, verbatim past-exam question. If asked whether there's more practice content beyond past papers, say that's on the roadmap, not shipped today.

## Audience
- **Primary:** CBSE Class 10 students (Maths & Science)
- **Secondary:** Parents, teachers/schools evaluating it for students, and — during beta — investors and content creators the company is in conversation with

## Values / positioning pillars
1. **Access for All** — personalised board prep shouldn't be limited to metro cities or students who can afford coaching
2. **Zero Friction** — browser-based, nothing to download, nothing new to learn
3. **Board-First Content** — every question is built around the actual CBSE exam pattern, no filler
4. **Honest & Transparent** — say exactly what's offered, what it costs, what results to expect — no false promises

## Voice & tone rules
- **Sell the benefit, don't state the restriction.** Say what the student gains, not what's gated. E.g. lead with "practice real CBSE PYQs instantly" rather than "requires login."
- **Use specific, entity-rich language**, not vague marketing-speak. Say "CBSE Class 10 Science PYQs, 2022–2026" and "case-based questions," not "practice questions" or "exam content."
- **No fabricated social proof.** Do not invent student testimonials, review quotes, or press mentions. If a post needs a quote or stat and none is confirmed real, flag that instead of inventing one — the site's own placeholder testimonials and placeholder press quotes are known issues being cleaned up, not a style to imitate.
- Match claims to actual product stage: it's in **beta**, not yet at scale — avoid implying large user numbers, extensive press coverage, or multi-board/multi-class coverage that don't exist yet.

## What NOT to claim
- No fake testimonials, review scores, or press logos/quotes
- No specific student count, pass-rate stats, or "X,000 students" claims (none are confirmed)
- No subjects/classes beyond CBSE Class 10 Maths & Science
- No claim of an app to download — it is intentionally browser-only
